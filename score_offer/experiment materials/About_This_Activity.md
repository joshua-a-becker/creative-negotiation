## About This Activity

In the main study, you would be assigned either the **Candidate** or **Recruiter** role and negotiate a job offer with one other participant. The role materials below describe your hypothetical interests and goals.

For this feedback activity, please read your assigned role and answer the questions that follow.

**Bonus payment for this feedback activity:** Among participants who pass our effort-screening checks, 20% will be selected at random to receive a bonus equal to the base payment—doubling their total payment. We aim to pay this within two days after the activity ends, and no later than seven days.

**In the main negotiation study:**

- **What determines how much I earn?** Your points from the final agreement determine your negotiation bonus: **1 point = US$1.00**. Without an agreement, you receive no negotiation bonus. Your base payment is unaffected.
- **How can we reach an agreement?** Both parties must officially accept the same offer in the system. Agreeing in chat is not enough. You may propose an offer worth zero or less, but each party can accept only an offer worth more than zero to them.
- **What happens if we don’t agree?** The Candidate continues their job search, and the Recruiter continues looking for a hire. Both receive **0 points**.
- **What should I do now?** Read your role information carefully. You may review it again while answering the questions.
